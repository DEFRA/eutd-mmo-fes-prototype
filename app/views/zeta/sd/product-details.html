Help with product and fishery net weights

The net weights can be found on the UK entry documents, or they may be calculated manually if not explicitly listed.

Net weight of the product on arrival:

This is the total weight of everything you received, including all ingredients and packaging. For example:

  • A box of fish pies (2kg)
  • Canned tuna in oil (0.5kg per tin)
  • Fish fillets in sauce (1.5kg)

Net weight of fishery products on arrival:

This is only the weight of the fish content, without any other ingredients such as brine, breadcrumbs or sauce. The fish weight cannot exceed the total product weight because the fish is part of the whole product.

For example:

  • The cod in a fish pie (0.8kg out of 2kg total)
  • The tuna in a tin (0.3kg out of 0.5kg total with oil)
  • The fillets without sauce (1.2kg out of 1.5kg total)

On departure:

If you export the same amount as you received, both "arrival" and "departure" weights will be the same. If you export only part of what you received, the departure weights will be lower.
