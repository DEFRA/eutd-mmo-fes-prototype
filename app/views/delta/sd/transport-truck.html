Truck arriving in the UK

You'll need to provide different numbers for the truck, trailer and container.

Country of departure
This is the country the consignment left before arriving in the UK.
Departure date
This is when the consignment left the country of departure. For example, 27/8/2025
Where the truck departs from
For example, Calais port, Transmarck logistics hub, or the place where the consignment left by truck before arriving in the UK
Place of unloading
This is where the consignment was unloaded from the truck when arriving in the UK.
Truck nationality
