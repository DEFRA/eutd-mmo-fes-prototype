Your catch certificate is being processed

Processing usually takes up to 30 minutes. Check your dashboard regularly for updates.

Once processing is complete, your submission will either be a successfully completed catch certificate or a failed draft that needs amending.

If it takes significantly longer than 30 minutes, contact support.

If your submission is successful

  1. Find the document in the 'Completed' list on your dashboard.

  2. Download the catch certificate by selecting 'View'. Do not amend the document.

  3. Email the catch certificate to the importer. The importer must submit it to the import control authority where your export enters the EU.

Help with viewing or downloading
  • If you are using Firefox as an internet browser, please ensure JavaScript is enabled in order to view and download the certificate.
  • If you are using a mobile device, please ensure you have installed a PDF viewer.

If your submission fails

  1. Find the document (labelled 'Failed') in the 'In progress' list on your dashboard.

  2. Select 'Continue' to view the validation failure reasons.

  3. Use the error messages and guidance to amend the document and resubmit.
